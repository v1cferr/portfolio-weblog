On November 19, 2026, Anthropic made its debut on the Nasdaq under the ticker
`ANT`. That same day I bought one share. Just one, for about US$ 98.

> This post is not investment advice. It is only the record of a personal
> decision.

## Why Anthropic

Because it makes a tool I use every day. When the company behind it went public,
I bought in.

## Why only one

Because I wanted to test it. I had never bought a share directly on the US
market, and I wanted to see in practice how that works through Inter, my
Brazilian bank, as an international purchase. One share was enough to go through
the whole process, from converting the money to the purchase.

## How it went

I liked it a lot. I bought through Inter itself, straight on the US market, and
paid less than 1% in fees on the currency conversion for investing.

Now I own a piece, a very small one, of the company that makes a tool I use
every day. And the test of buying straight on the US market worked.
