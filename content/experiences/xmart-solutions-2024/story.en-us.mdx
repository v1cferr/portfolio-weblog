XGuardian was my first job as a developer. I worked on it from June 2024 to
November 2025, in three positions.

## The problem

XGuardian is the application security platform of Xmart Solutions. Its public
website describes it as a place that centralizes AppSec signals (SAST, SCA,
DAST, containers, IaC, secrets, SBOM and reports) to support visibility and risk
prioritization.

The part I worked on is bringing those security scans into the development
workflow.

## My role, in three positions

- **Frontend Developer Intern**, June to September 2024, on-site in Santo André.
  I built interfaces with React, Vite and TypeScript, helped with front-end
  improvements to XGuardian products and internal tools, and worked with
  developers, designers and product people on UI components that were easy to
  maintain.
- **Full Stack Developer**, September 2024 to April 2025, on-site and full-time
  in Santo André.
- **Software Developer**, April to November 2025, freelance and remote from São
  Carlos. Back-end development and maintenance of XGuardian products, automation
  and DevOps tasks for the development and deployment workflows, and technical
  improvements to internal and external components.

Across all three, planning ran in Scrumban with Jira, and the work was
documented in Confluence.

## What I worked on

### Pipelines that run the scans

On the CI/CD side, I automated SAST and SCA scans with GitHub Actions, writing
the automation in Shell and YAML. Running the scans in the pipeline cut the
manual configuration they needed, and the GitHub Actions automation shortened
deploys.

I also helped maintain CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins, GitHub and shell scripts,
including Jenkins pipelines that block on tests, linting and formatting, and
worked with AWS services for deployment and environment automation.

### The front end

The front end was React, Vite and TypeScript. Besides fixing bugs and building
new features, I optimized the requests it made, which made it faster. I also
contributed to internal tools and interactive dashboards.

### The back end

On the back end I built and maintained services in Python with FastAPI, and
contributed to the vulnerability analysis workflows: SAST, SCA, DAST and
container analysis.

### A VS Code extension

I built a VS Code extension on the VS Code API with Node.js. The XGuardian
extension on the Visual Studio Marketplace runs SAST and SCA scans from inside
the editor.

## What I learned

- **Pipelines and CI/CD.** Including Jenkins pipelines that block a change on
  tests, linting and formatting.
- **Application security scanning.** SAST, SCA, DAST and container scanning,
  vulnerability analysis, and dealing with false positives.
- **DevSecOps as a practice, not a tool.**
- **Teamwork and agile planning.**
